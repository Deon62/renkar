Our mission

To deliver safe, reliable, and delightful travel experiences by combining human-centered service with smart technology. Renkar empowers customers to book quality cars and professional drivers within minutes, and gives drivers predictable work with fair earnings.

Safety first

Every driver is background checked, every vehicle inspected, and every ride tracked end-to-end for peace of mind.

Payments that work

We support M-Pesa, Airtel Money, and cards so renters can pay their way—and drivers get paid on time.

Smart operations

Real-time fleet monitoring, AI-powered dispatching, and predictive maintenance keep our city moving smoothly.

What we value

Trust

Transparency from booking to billing with honest pricing, verified partners, and responsive support.

Sustainable growth

We partner with owners, SaaS platforms, and community groups to build an ecosystem where everyone thrives.

Hospitality

Renkar drivers are trained to deliver warm, professional service that feels premium every time.

The Renkar journey

  • 2019

    Renkar launches in Nairobi with a small fleet of 20 cars servicing airport transfers.

  • 2021

    Introduced verified driver marketplace and expanded to 5 major Kenyan cities.

  • 2023

    Raised Series A funding to scale operations, enhance the app, and add corporate travel solutions.

  • Today

    Serving thousands of daily rides, powering business logistics, and exploring East African expansion.
